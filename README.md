# 🏍️ Love Bike

> **Short Description:** Ride the Love Bike, hit Dilly Dally challenges, and collect Vibes across neon cities before time runs out.

Inspired by the hit track **"Love Bike" by CMA Records**, jump on the scooter with your partner and ride through glowing neon cities filled with romance, rhythm, and high-energy electronic beats. 

---

## 🎮 Game Overview
Your goal is to complete the full journey in **4 minutes** flat. Along the way, watch out for the **Dilly Dally Challenge** signs. Whenever you see one, perform the move to keep your rhythm alive and earn maximum points!

### ✨ Key Features & Scoring:
* **Romantic Atmosphere:** Beautiful neon-synthwave city landscapes syncing with a romantic club aesthetic.
* **Dilly Dally Moves:** Every successful challenge gives you **+25 Vibe**.
* **Exploration Bonus:** Earn extra Vibes automatically just by surviving and reaching new hidden locations during your ride.
* **Smooth Physics:** Tight, responsive steering designed perfectly for fast-paced mobile runs.

*Feel the music. Feel the vibe. Ride the Love Bike.*

## 📥 How to Install and Play on Android

Because this game is shared directly with fans via GitHub, your phone might display an "Unknown Source" warning. Follow these quick steps to install safely:

1. Go to the **Releases** section on the right side of this page.
2. Download the latest version of the `Love_Bike_Game.apk` file directly onto your Android device.
3. Open the downloaded file. If a security prompt pops up, click **"Settings"** and toggle on **"Allow from this source"** (or click **"Install Anyway"** on Play Protect).
4. Launch the game, tap **GO**, and enjoy the ride!
